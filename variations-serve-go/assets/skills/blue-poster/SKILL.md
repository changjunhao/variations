# Minimal Blue Poster · 蓝色光波

Compile one final image-generation prompt that turns the user-provided photograph into a finished collectible Minimal Blue Poster: a full-page warm-ivory architectural print study where the source survives only as line, structure and rhythm, and 3–6 translucent blue optical carriers carry exactly one coherent effect program.

## Contract

- **Canvas:** orientation-following, flat and front-facing, never a framed mockup. Portrait or square source → one native exact 3:4 portrait page; landscape source → one native exact 4:3 landscape page (see Landscape Adaptation). If the server supplies a measured source size and orientation, adopt it and never guess.
- **Input role:** the photograph is only a semantic and structural source. Extract the subject's recognizable silhouette, central axis, internal structure, repeating rhythm and important environmental geometry. Do not preserve photographic lighting, photo texture, realistic scene rendering, the original crop or any photographic window. Translate source color into restrained print material.
- **Foundation across the entire canvas:** warm ivory uncoated paper with subtle tooth and generous negative space; the source subject and environment reconstructed as one complete, continuous pale-gray graphite architectural or structural drawing; the main subject compositionally dominant and immediately recognizable; the secondary environment as quieter technical linework and sparse construction geometry; the drawing visible outside and beneath every effect carrier; at least 40% untouched ivory paper and one clear breathing corridor around the primary subject.
- **Carrier system:** 3–6 visibly active optical carriers — 1–2 large anchors, 1–3 medium supports, at most 1 small accent — arranged as no more than two coherent clusters tied to the source's structural axes, perspective or material zones. Attach every small carrier to a cluster or crop it at a page edge; never scatter isolated floating rectangles through open space. Let at least one carrier crop beyond a page edge or dissolve without a fully closed outline. Every active carrier must process the matching local fragment of the underlying subject or environment drawing. Translucent overlaps behave as restrained physical-paper multiplication, never opaque blocks; no empty cards, no unrelated imagery, no photographic fragments. Never exceed six visibly bounded effect regions.
- **Effect program:** exactly one of S01–S11 below. Default: choose the program that best fits the photograph's character (see Program Matching). The instruction axis 效果程序 overrides the default when the user names a program (accept Chinese name, English name or S-number, e.g. 蓝晒光学版 / Cyanotype Optical Plates / S06).
- **Palette and material:** warm ivory paper, pale graphite gray, one restrained dark-blue family abstracted from the source (Prussian blue, navy, cobalt), and only the microscopic registration accents the selected program specifies (coral, vermilion, yellow or cyan micro-marks). Premium independent architecture and art zine, experimental print studio, tactile vellum, museum-grade, quiet but materially rich.
- **Text:** strictly none. No readable title, caption, logo, watermark, letter, numeral, sign or label of any kind.

## Effect Programs (S01–S11)

- **S01 Blue Exposure Laboratory 蓝色曝光实验室:** large vertical carriers translate matching structural fragments into translucent Prussian-blue cyanotype exposures; medium carriers apply deterministic navy stippling that follows local shadow density and spatial depth; small satellites are pale negative exposures with luminous drawn edges; overlaps become darker physical exposure bands; the subject passes through one nearly colorless frame with only one precise blue exposed edge.
- **S02 Optical Field Array 光场阵列:** large carriers render disciplined navy parallel lines that bend according to underlying form and depth; medium carriers cross a second line frequency to create controlled moiré interference pockets; small carriers use widely spaced echo contours around defining edges; overlaps intensify interference while paper remains visible; direct line motion toward the composition's main axis or vanishing point.
- **S03 EdgeLoom Effect Sampler 织边效果取样器:** give each carrier one distinct but coherent print process — Riso overprint, cyanotype relief, 1-bit Bayer, chromatic halftone, stable stippling, op-art flow, woodcut hatch, liquid chrome, print glitch — never repeating an effect; the primary subject receives the quietest fine-line treatment; stronger cobalt, cyan, coral and muted-magenta treatments stay toward the sides; the result is one artwork, not thumbnails or a filter menu.
- **S04 Quiet Effect Cabinet 静谧效果陈列柜:** give each active carrier one material — cyanotype wash, ultrafine cobalt halftone, navy moiré contour, graphite hatch, blind-emboss relief; unequal sizes and offsets with at most two restrained overlaps; the subject in whisper-light pencil with a clean breathing channel; one tiny yellow-orange registration ring.
- **S05 Ink Grid Interference 墨线网格干涉:** reinforce the subject and matching fragments inside selected carriers with expressive but airy black graphite and technical-pen lines; one dominant optical carrier toward a secondary side zone filled with fine navy moiré topographic contours; two pale blueprint-grid carriers on the opposite side dissolving before the margins; one muted cyan disk and one vermilion crosshair as microscopic accents; one dense optical zone against broad silence.
- **S06 Cyanotype Optical Plates 蓝晒光学版:** carriers in one restrained Prussian-blue family alternate foggy cyanotype exposure, stippled photographic grain translated into print, extremely fine moiré contour waves and nearly clear glass-blue wash; two carriers extend beyond side edges and three overlap while protecting the subject corridor; fine black graphite accents only on the subject; one tiny unfilled golden-yellow ring; handcrafted optical plates, never digital UI cards.
- **S07 Registration Weather 套准气象:** carriers become atmospheric samples with deliberately unequal visual weight (one dominant, medium, nearly disappearing): misty cyanotype bloom, sparse stipple rain, dry graphite fog, faint halftone atmosphere, washed blue emulsion, blind emboss, one narrow moiré pressure field; four tiny coral-and-cyan registration coordinates and one short yellow datum line; the feel of an architect's atmospheric research archive.
- **S08 Material Tectonics 材料建构:** the largest carriers carry five distinct material strata — Prussian-blue cyanotype grain, ultrafine black stipple, directional graphite hatch, soft blind-emboss relief, dense navy moiré lines — in a descending upper-left to lower-right tectonic rhythm with two plates partially cropped at the side edges; effects clip sharply at rectangle boundaries; one tiny coral square and two microscopic cobalt alignment crosses; one deliberately strong lower-right moiré plate; at least 30% untouched ivory paper.
- **S09 Monochrome Data Garden 单色数据花园:** near-monochrome ink black, graphite and desaturated blue-gray; vertical carriers carry hairline moiré waves, soft cyanotype stains and wind-shaped organic stipple particles; density concentrates in two opposing edge zones while the subject stays calm and technically drawn; one pale-blue circle and one short black datum line; effects may grow organically inside their windows but clip at outer boundaries.
- **S10 Selected Synthesis 精选合成:** one side activates three asymmetrical cyanotype and stipple exposure carriers echoing the source's curved or organic rhythm; the opposite side activates three transparent optical carriers filled with fine navy moiré and topographic waves echoing rectilinear structure; a faint cobalt blueprint grid and construction rays persist beneath all frames; only two controlled overlaps near the edges; one translucent powder-blue circle, one coral registration square and two tiny cobalt crosses.
- **S11 Cyanotype Ma Registry 蓝晒留白登记册:** 4–6 translucent Prussian-blue cyanotype exposure rectangles clustered asymmetrically near the left and right edges; fine stipple grain inside only two carriers; a few cobalt construction lines converging toward the subject; the subject mostly unframed, pale graphite and quiet; exactly one tiny coral-red registration square; 35–45% untouched ivory paper.

## Carrier Override Rule

The 3–6 carrier budget overrides every higher rectangle count or one-rectangle-per-process instruction inside the selected effect program. If the program names more processes than the budget allows, combine compatible processes inside shared continuous carriers without adding borders or panels. Never exceed six visibly bounded effect regions. Per-program untouched-paper floors replace the default 40% only where stated (S08 ≥30%, S11 35–45%).

## Landscape Adaptation (4:3)

For a landscape source, keep the identical foundation, carrier budget, effect program and palette, and reflow the page horizontally: tie the two clusters to the left/right structural zones instead of upper/lower ones, let large anchors read as vertical bands at the sides or horizontal bands above/below the subject as the geometry demands, and keep the continuous subject corridor and the untouched-paper floor. Never split the page into stacked zones and never letterbox a portrait composition inside a landscape raster.

## Program Matching (default selection)

- Strong linear or rectilinear architecture, towers, grids → S02, S05 or S10.
- Organic nature subjects (trees, water, hills, fog-free landscapes) → S01, S06 or S09.
- Atmospheric scenes (fog, haze, sky, sea, weather) → S07.
- Dense material or texture subjects (facades, interiors, structural close-ups) → S04 or S08.
- Mixed curved plus rectilinear geometry (wheels, domes, stations) → S10 or S02.
- Quiet minimal subjects with large negative space → S11 or S03.
- Varied material zones that benefit from several distinct print processes → S03.

Choose one program and commit; never blend two programs.

## Prompt Compiler

Before writing the final prompt, identify: the core subject and its central axis; the structural rhythm and environmental geometry worth reconstructing; the selected effect program; the carrier plan (count, sizes, cluster placement, which local fragment each carrier processes, which carrier crops or dissolves); the microscopic registration accents the program allows.

Write the final image prompt as four compact paragraphs:

1. **Canvas and foundation:** orientation and exact ratio (3:4 portrait or 4:3 landscape), warm ivory uncoated paper with subtle tooth, the whole page as one continuous pale-gray graphite architectural drawing of the source, drawing visible beneath every carrier, ≥40% untouched paper and a clear breathing corridor around the subject.
2. **Subject and carriers:** what stays recognizable (silhouette, axis, internal structure, rhythm), the carrier count and cluster arrangement tied to the source's axes, crop/dissolve behavior, and that every carrier processes the matching local fragment with translucent physical-paper overlaps.
3. **Effect program:** the selected program by name with its processes mapped onto the named carriers, plus its exact microscopic registration accents.
4. **Material, silence and avoids:** palette and premium print-studio materiality; completely textless; the avoid list; the final check.

Prefer concrete visual instructions over a long style essay. State carrier placements concretely (e.g. "one large vertical cyanotype plate cropped at the left edge over the tower's lattice, one medium stipple plate upper-right over the sky") instead of abstract adjectives.

## Preventive Constraints

Write these into the prompt as explicit bans — they have no second chance:

- any photographic-looking area, preserved photo window, realistic sky, photographic lighting, glowing or realistically colored subject, photo collage, original crop imitation;
- horizontal split, two-zone before-and-after composition, stacked zones, letterboxing;
- more than six visibly bounded effect regions; isolated small floating rectangles; evenly scattered panels; regular UI grid; dashboard cards; scrapbook; empty rectangles; repeated framing across the primary subject;
- neon rainbow, cyberpunk, cartoon, glossy CGI, cheap vector art, poster mockup;
- any readable text, title, caption, logo, watermark, letter, numeral or sign;
- distorted or illegible subject; any ratio other than the selected 3:4 or 4:3.

## Final Self-Check

Before returning the compiled prompt, verify:

- Is the canvas stated as exact 3:4 portrait (or 4:3 landscape for a landscape source), flat and front-facing, adopting the server-measured orientation when present?
- Is the source reconstructed as one continuous pale-graphite drawing with the subject dominant and recognizable through line, structure and rhythm?
- Are there 3–6 visibly active carriers in no more than two coherent clusters, no isolated small rectangle, at least one carrier cropped or dissolved, and a continuous subject corridor?
- Is exactly one effect program present, with every carrier processing the matching local fragment?
- Does the page preserve the untouched-paper floor (default ≥40%)?
- Are the only colors ivory, pale graphite, one dark-blue family and the program's microscopic accents?
- Is the composition completely textless and free of photographic regions, splits, UI grids and mockup framing?
- Will the poster still read at thumbnail size?
