# Scenic Postcard Editorial Style System

## Overall aesthetic

Minimal editorial poster, poetic city illustration, contemporary art book aesthetic, Scandinavian minimalism, Japanese editorial design, soft gouache illustration, abstract cityscape, quiet luxury poster, refined print design. The finished work should feel like an independent art magazine inner page, a city travel photobook spread, a contemporary art book cover, or a museum shop poster: refined, restrained, quiet, poetic, with paper-print texture. The illustrated region is always an artistic distillation of the photographic region.

## Two-part composition

Orientation-adaptive two-part composition: for a landscape or near-square source, split into an upper photographic region and a lower illustrated region; for a portrait source (aspect at most 0.8), place the photographic region on the left and the illustrated region on the right — never stack a portrait photo above the illustration on a tall skinny canvas. The two regions are equal in size: the canvas is exactly the photograph doubled along the joining axis.

- The photographic region keeps the original real photograph and its original composition: sky, buildings, and main subject elements stay in place and unchanged in content.
- The illustrated region is derived from the photograph: its most recognizable elements are extracted and simplified into minimal art illustration.
- The two regions share one coherent palette so the poster reads as a single printed object.

## Photographic region

- Keep the real photographic texture; apply only a slight cinematic color grade.
- Lower saturation; keep the tone soft, restrained, and quiet.
- Dominant tones: blue-grey, warm yellow, ivory and other low-saturation natural colors.
- Forbidden: excessive HDR, strong sharpening, glossy retouching, any AI-repaint look, content changes, or reframing of the scene.

## Gouache distillation

- Extract the most recognizable elements from the photo; do not replicate it pixel by pixel.
- Simplify buildings into geometric color blocks; compress distant views into thin rectangular silhouettes.
- Illustration texture: soft gouache, dry-brush texture, watercolor-like opacity, subtle paper grain, hand-painted editorial illustration, matte printed texture.
- Forbidden: visible outlines, cartoon look, cyberpunk, 3D rendering, vector-icon gloss.
- Keep generous warm-ivory negative space; the layout stays sparse, balanced, quiet, poetic, and modern.

## Palette

Overall: warm ivory, muted navy, blue grey, smoky grey, warm ochre, mustard yellow, soft beige; low saturation throughout. Derive hues from the photograph itself and mute them; prefer one dominant tone, one dark structural tone, and one or two small warm accents. Never add neon or unsupported saturated colors.

## Typography

- Generate one English main title and one subtitle from the image content: the title names the mood or scene in two to five words; the subtitle adds one quiet detail in three to seven words.
- Main title: elegant classic serif, dark blue-grey color.
- Subtitle: fine italic serif / editorial italic, clearly smaller than the title.
- Left-aligned, restrained font sizes, with ample breathing space around the text; keep text away from the illustration masses and the canvas edges.
- No extra words, dates, coordinates, place-label clutter, logos, or watermarks.

## Acceptance criteria

The final work contains exactly:

1. one photographic region faithful to the source photograph, quietly graded;
2. one gouache distillation derived from the same photo;
3. one English main title and at most one subtitle;
4. dominant ivory negative space in the illustrated region.

Reject any result containing cartoon or vector-icon styling, outlines on illustration shapes, 3D effects, HDR or over-sharpened photo regions, redrawn photographic content, dense decoration, extra text beyond title and subtitle, watermarks, or glossy digital texture.
