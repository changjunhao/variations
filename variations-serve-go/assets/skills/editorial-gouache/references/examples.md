# Scene Examples

- **Street or campus:** keep one landmark, the street/path direction, one vertical rhythm, and at most one scale figure; remove cars, signs, and façade texture.
- **Architecture or interior:** keep the signature mass, perspective, and one repeated motif; reduce windows, furniture, lamps, columns, or stairs to bars, blocks, and curves.
- **Landscape:** keep the ridgeline, depth relationship, and two or three silhouettes; merge foliage and terrain into broad washes.
- **Portrait:** keep head angle, pose, hairstyle/glasses, garment mass, and gesture; remove facial and clothing micro-detail.
- **Vehicle:** keep travel direction, silhouette, characteristic curve, and essential structure; remove mechanical detail.

For every scene, keep the source immediately recognizable, emphasize one visual idea, use 3–5 colors, and finish with an irregular open painted footprint surrounded by roughly 15–25% natural paper white.
