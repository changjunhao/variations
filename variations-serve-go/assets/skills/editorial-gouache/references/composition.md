# Composition

Recompose without severing recognition. Preserve the source's orientation, direction, anchor order, characteristic curves, and essential adjacency; then adjust crop, spacing, scale, and emphasis.

Choose one clear idea: enlarge a foreground form, repeat a structural motif, expand a quiet field, crop architecture, or isolate a color accent. Do not stack several effects.

## Open painted footprint

- Let the painted cluster occupy about 70–85% of the sheet by default.
- Leave about 15–25% natural paper white around and through the image.
- Place the cluster centrally or off-center as the subject requires; avoid automatic centering.
- Let neighboring forms end independently. Use unequal wash lengths, tapered ends, dry skips, lost edges, and a few marks that approach or touch an edge.
- Keep the perimeter asymmetric and organically irregular.

Paper white must enter the composition naturally; it is not a mat or an added border. Reject full bleed, a sharp rectangular footprint, a uniform margin, an oval/circular vignette, a fuzzy halo, torn-paper contours, and evenly distributed splatter.
