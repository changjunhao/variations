# Simplification

## Keep

Retain only:

- 1–3 identity anchors: landmark silhouette, pose, vehicle, awning, bridge, façade, or other unmistakable feature;
- one structural rhythm: columns, arches, windows, stems, poles, branches, stairs, or rooflines;
- spatial invariants: orientation, facing/travel direction, left-right order, defining curve/slope, and essential adjacency;
- 3–5 remembered color families;
- optionally one tiny figure for scale.

## Remove

Delete or merge roughly 50–70% of photographic information, starting with texture, repeated micro-detail, clutter, secondary people/vehicles, signage, minor shadows, and realistic material description.

Translate retained objects into visual shorthand: trees become a few masses; flowers become cups and stems; façades become blocks and bars; arches become confident curves; crowds become small marks; vehicles become a silhouette and a few structural divisions.

Stop simplifying before the source identity or spatial direction is lost. Recognition should come from selected anchors, not from preserving every object.
