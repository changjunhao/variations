# Color Palette

Compress the source into 3–5 pigment families plus warm paper white. Assign colors by role: dominant field, secondary field, structural dark, and optional accent.

Useful families include indigo/navy/cobalt, forest or moss green, coral/vermilion/pink, ochre/mustard/warm beige, plum, and near-black. Treat diluted and darkened versions as one family.

Keep colors luminous and optically clear. Use water, pooling, and transparent overlap for variation; use opaque gouache only at the focal point and structural darks. Avoid copying every source color, equal saturation everywhere, smooth digital gradients, photographic color noise, and muddy gray-brown overmixing.
