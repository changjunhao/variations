# Enamel Pin Travel Poster Style System

## Overall aesthetic

Quiet luxury, premium travel-magazine editorial: matte finish, soft even light, generous negative space, restrained palette. The poster reads as one printed object — a matte panel seamlessly joined above the original photograph — never a collage, a sticker sheet, or a souvenir-shop flyer.

## Fixed template geometry

Strict portrait canvas, identical on every output:

1. **Upper panel** — about 45% of total height, full width, matte mid-tone blue-violet or grey-blue, flat and calm.
2. **Pin** — centered horizontally on the panel, sitting in its upper half; small relative to the panel (roughly a third of panel width), with generous breathing room around it.
3. **Caption block** — centered on the panel below the pin: city line, ornament line, country line, with even spacing.
4. **Photo region** — the remaining lower part, full width: the original photograph reproduced faithfully — same people, scenery, composition and color grading, no crop, no restyle, no added effects.

Pin scale, caption position and spacing never drift between outputs.

## Panel

- Matte mid-tone blue-violet or grey-blue, derived from the photo's own palette but muted.
- Never near-black, never bright, never saturated; light enough that pin and type read clearly.
- Perfectly flat color — no gradients, no texture noise, no vignette.

## Enamel pin

- One irregularly shaped fridge magnet; the silhouette is organic, echoing the scene's contour — never a plain rectangle, circle or shield by default.
- Exquisite cloisonné enamel texture: smooth colored enamel cells, fine recessed detail, soft inner shading.
- Only a subtle thin metal rim outlines the shape — never glossy chrome, never chunky gold, never dark or heavy.
- Inside the pin: one to three iconic elements of the photographed destination (landmark, terrain, coastline, transit, vegetation), distilled and elegant.
- When the photo's person is readable and the composition benefits, the person appears inside the pin as a small enamel figure faithful to their pose and outfit; otherwise the pin carries scenery only.
- Keep the pin scene sparse and iconic: no crowds, no clutter, no text inside the pin.

## Caption typography

- Exactly two text lines: city name on top, country name below; all caps.
- High-end hairline serif, wide letter-spacing; the city line noticeably larger than the country line.
- Between the lines: one thin horizontal rule, broken at center by one small diamond ornament.
- Ink color harmonizes with the panel (soft ivory or muted gold); calm contrast, never harsh.
- Place names use their local spelling with proper diacritics (e.g. REYKJAVÍK) unless the instruction specifies otherwise.
- No other text anywhere: no slogans, dates, coordinates, initials, logos or watermarks.

## Acceptance criteria

The final image contains exactly:

1. an upper matte blue-violet or grey-blue panel at about 45% of the height;
2. one small centered irregular enamel pin of the destination with a subtle thin metal rim;
3. a two-line serif caption — city over country — with a thin rule and one small diamond between;
4. the original photograph below, untouched in people, scenery, composition and color.

Reject any result with a saturated or near-black panel; a glossy, chrome or oversized pin; extra text, badges, stamps or watermarks; a cropped, recolored or recomposed photo region; a cartoon, sticker or flat-vector pin; or template geometry that drifts from the fixed layout.
