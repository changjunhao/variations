# Paper Diorama Postcard Style System

## Overall aesthetic

A highly detailed realistic photograph of a handcrafted keepsake, shot on location: luxury travel-agency aesthetic, travel-magazine cover quality, warm natural light, shallow depth of field, cinematic color grading, realistic shadows, premium paper texture, handmade detail, photorealistic, 8k, masterpiece. The finished work must feel like a real object photographed with a real camera — never a digital collage, a 3D render, or an illustrated poster. The mood is hidden-gem, dreamy, fairytale-like travel; avoid generic tourism energy entirely.

## Four-layer depth hierarchy

The composition follows one strict depth order, viewed from a slightly elevated angle on a square canvas:

1. **Blurred reality** — the source photograph as the real destination scene in the far background: recognizable but softly defocused, creamy bokeh, quiet and atmospheric.
2. **Sharp postcard** — the vintage postcard in the foreground plane, perfectly crisp, carrying the stamp, postmark and handwritten note.
3. **Emerging diorama** — the 3D paper-cut landscape rising from the postcard surface toward the camera, its layered strata catching realistic paper shadows.
4. **Traveler figure** — the collector-grade character standing inside the diorama, the sharpest narrative focus within the miniature world.

Keep the postcard sharp while the background melts; this contrast is the link between reality and imagination.

## Postcard

- Authentic vintage postcard: rounded corners, visible quality paper grain, slightly warm aged tone.
- One postage stamp and one elegant postmark; one destination stamp or small ink illustration may complement but never crowd.
- One short handwritten travel note in an elegant hand — personal, poetic, destination-specific, expressing discovery, wonder or a secret-journey feeling.
- No extra text, addresses, numbers, logos, or watermarks.

## Paper diorama

- Built from one to three iconic elements of the photographed destination: landmarks, layered paper-cut terrain, local architecture style, vegetation, coastline.
- Architecture must reflect the destination's building language; color tone is drawn from the source photograph.
- Ultra-fine paper-cut structure: crisp cut edges, stacked paper strata for distance, delicate layering, realistic cast shadows between layers.
- Every element emerges naturally from the postcard surface — nothing floats disconnected above the card.
- Keep the scene sparse and iconic: no crowds, no generic tourist props, no dense miniature clutter.

## Traveler figure

- Collector-grade figurine quality: realistic proportions, expressive pose, cinematic styling.
- Fashionable travel outfit inspired by the local climate and culture; subtle narrative accessories (camera, map, satchel, lantern) that tell a small story.
- Stands naturally within the diorama at miniature scale, grounded on the paper terrain with a believable shadow.
- Never cartoon, toy-like, or CGI-rendered; the figure reads as a premium handcrafted collectible.

## Acceptance criteria

The final image contains exactly:

1. the destination photograph as a creamy-bokeh background;
2. one sharp vintage postcard with stamp, postmark and one short handwritten note;
3. one emerging paper diorama of one to three iconic destination elements;
4. at most one collector-grade traveler figure inside the diorama.

Reject any result containing a sharp background, more than three landmarks, crowded or generic tourist scenes, cluttered text, cartoon or CGI figures, floating paper elements, flat 2D illustration dioramas, heavy HDR, or strong sharpening.
